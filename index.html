Our Story

A family kitchen that grew into a restaurant

Aheer's Kitchen started with one simple idea: serve the food people grew up eating, cooked the slow, honest way. Every karahi is made to order, every kabab is grilled over charcoal, and every naan comes straight from the tandoor.

Founded and owned by Basit Aheer and led day-to-day by our CEO Ahmad, we believe a good meal should feel like being at home, just with more flavour.

  • 🌿 Fresh daily – no frozen shortcuts
  • 🔥 Real charcoal – for that smoky BBQ taste
  • 🤝 Honest prices – generous portions
